Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Driver Fatigue Tracker ("the App") is designed to help users monitor driving activity, vehicle speed, continuous driving duration, and break periods.

Information We Collect

The App may access the device's location information, including location information while the App is running in the background. Location data is used to detect driving activity and monitor vehicle speed and driving duration.

The App does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other account information.

How We Use Location Information

Location information is used only for the core functionality of the App, including driving detection, speed monitoring, continuous driving time tracking, and break monitoring.

Data Storage

The App may store information locally on the user's device for features such as compliance and driving history. The App does not sell personal information or location information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the osmdroid library for map-related functionality. The App does not use advertising services or Advertising ID for personalized advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information or location data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by the App. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
